## Why a CLA exists

Because no license is granted, ownership of any accepted contribution has to be
made unambiguous. Without a CLA, a contributor could later assert a copyright
interest in code merged into this repository, which would complicate both
enforcement of the notice in `LICENSE` and any future sale or licensing of the
project.

## Requirements to contribute

1. You must have the legal right to submit the contribution, and it must be
   your own original work — not copied from another project.
2. You must sign this CLA. Signing is done by opening a pull request that adds
   a comment containing the exact acceptance text below, or by emailing the
   copyright holder with that text and the pull request URL.
3. On acceptance, you assign copyright in your contribution to the copyright
   holder, and you agree not to assert any other rights in it.

## Acceptance text

CLA.md. I confirm that my contribution is my own original work, that I have
the right to submit it under the terms of CLA.md, and that I assign all
copyright in that contribution to the copyright holder of limbo-c++.

## No unsolicited contributions

Pull requests submitted without a signed CLA **may not be merged**, and confer
no license to their authors. Code in such a pull request is not granted any
right to be used here, and its submission grants no rights to the submitter.
